# May the take-over stop adding guest lines to an account cart that holds items, as an existing unit test expects today?

## Question

The story wants "Continue" on the take-over page to ask again when the account's cart filled meanwhile. The take-over
form's only port, `RecoverCartUseCase`, adds the guest lines to an account cart that holds items
(`src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/cart/application/cartrecovery/recovercart/RecoverCartUseCase.java:83`),
and one existing test asserts exactly that:

- `src/test/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/cart/application/cartrecovery/recovercart/RecoverCartUseCaseTest.java:84`
  `addsTheAnonymousItemsToACartTheAccountAlreadyHas` — today: the guest line is added, the account cart holds 2 lines.
  The story would need instead: the take-over is refused, both carts as they were.

The story has no `## Changed expectations`; its answered assumption ("nothing is merged without a choice") says the
same in other words, but the pipeline lets a stage change a test only on a changed-expectation line or a decision.
No other caller of `RecoverCartInputPort` exists (`CartMergePageController.java:143` is the only one).

## Options
- a: The take-over refuses an account cart that holds items, with a named failure the merge page answers by showing both carts.
  `RecoverCartUseCaseTest#addsTheAnonymousItemsToACartTheAccountAlreadyHas` changes to assert the refusal and both carts
  unchanged; the merge branch of `RecoverCartUseCase` goes, since no caller is left for it.
- b: The command carries what the page showed (an account cart that held nothing), and only then is the take-over refused.
  The test stays as it is, but it then keeps alive a merge branch no caller takes.
- c: The controller asks for the merge options again before it sends the take-over.
  No test changes, but the decision sits in the adapter and the window between read and change stays open
  (catalog `pitfall/a-command-decided-on-an-earlier-read.md`).

## Recommendation

a — the catalog's pitfall puts the re-check into the command itself, and the person's answered assumption already says
nothing is merged without a choice; the test's expectation is the very miss the story closes.
