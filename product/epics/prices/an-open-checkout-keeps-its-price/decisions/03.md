# Place Order now confirms at today's total, but the payment was authorized for the old one — what happens to the payment?

## Question

The judge asked. The payment step has the provider authorize the session's total at that moment
(`src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/checkout/application/checkoutcompletion/submitpayment/SubmitPaymentUseCase.java:99-109`),
and the model holds that an authorized payment stands for a total only when its amount equals it
(`PaymentSelection.covers`, glossary "authorized amount"). Before this story a changed price refused Place Order,
so the confirmed total always equalled the authorized one. Now `CheckoutSession.confirm` replaces the lines and
totals with today's (`src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/checkout/domain/model/CheckoutSession.java:428-430`)
and nothing compares them with the authorization: in `place-order-after-the-refusal-orders-what-the-review-now-shows`
the order is confirmed at 54.98 EUR with 54.99 EUR authorized, and a price rise would confirm an order above what
the provider authorized. The fingerprint carries only the payment provider, not the authorized amount. The story
does not say which should happen, and each answer changes what the customer sees.

## Options

- a: Place Order with a total the authorized payment does not cover is refused and sends the guest back to the payment step.
  Keeps the model's rule; criterion 4 then needs a payment step between the refusal and the confirmation, and the
  story's scenarios change.
- b: Place Order asks the provider for a new authorization of today's total, releasing the old one, before it confirms.
  Criterion 4 stays as written; the confirmation reaches the payment provider, which can be unavailable or refuse,
  and those refusals need wording and criteria.
- c: The order is confirmed at today's total and the authorized amount may differ (settled at capture).
  No new step; the rule "an authorized payment covers exactly the total" no longer holds after confirmation, and
  the glossary and the .NET shop must say so.

## Recommendation

a — the customer pays exactly what they saw without the shop silently changing an authorization, and the payment
step already knows how to authorize a changed total; it costs one more step in criterion 4's journey.

## Answer
answer: correction: none of a/b/c — the payment is authorized at "Place Order", after the fingerprint and the stock are checked, for exactly the total the review showed; the payment step only chooses how to pay. The confirmation shows the prices of the moment the order was placed and nothing changes them afterwards. Written as its own story, `the-payment-is-authorized-at-place-order` (epic provider-payment), which this story now depends on; this story gains: a refused "Place Order" requests no payment, and the order after the refusal is authorized at the total then shown.
