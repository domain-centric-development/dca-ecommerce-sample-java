# Does the story's spec go to the feature `assortment` — should its front matter carry `feature: assortment`?

## Question

The story names no `feature:` in its front matter, so the plan skeleton sends its criteria to the
context's own spec, `product/spec/product/scenarios.md`: nine criteria that stand today in
`product/spec/product/assortment/scenarios.md` (`a-lower-case-sku-is-refused`, `a-blank-name-is-refused`,
`an-overlong-name-is-refused`, `an-overlong-description-is-refused`, `an-overlong-image-url-is-refused`,
`a-price-of-zero-is-refused`, `a-price-that-rounds-to-zero-is-refused`, `a-price-above-the-highest-is-refused`,
`a-negative-initial-stock-is-refused`) would move out of it, and the five new ones would land beside them.
The story's own Notes say the opposite: "Spec (`product/assortment`): changes the refusal scenarios under
their rules; adds …". The moved criteria would also leave their rules split across two files: "A name is
required and at most 255 characters long" keeps `a-name-of-255-characters-is-accepted` in assortment, "A
price is at most 999999999999.99" keeps `the-highest-price-is-accepted`, "Initial stock may be zero but not
negative" keeps `an-initial-stock-of-zero-is-accepted`. The story file is the person's; a stage may not add
the key.

Evidence: the story's front matter (no `feature:` line) and its `## Notes`; the skeleton's lines
`- moves <key>: assortment → context` in the run's plan.md; `product/spec/product/assortment/scenarios.md`
(the nine keys and their rules); sibling stories in the same epic carry the key, e.g.
`shop-api/an-unusable-cart-request-is-a-problem-document/story.md` (`feature: shopping`).

## Options
- a: Add `feature: assortment` to the story's front matter; the criteria change in place in the assortment spec.
  The plan is otherwise complete and is re-run unchanged apart from the spec delta's move lines.
- b: Leave the story as it is; the nine criteria move to `product/spec/product/scenarios.md` and the five new ones
  land there, splitting the four rules across two spec files.

## Recommendation
a — the story's Notes already name `product/assortment`, and the rules the criteria stand under keep other
criteria there; the missing key reads as an omission, not a decision to move them.
