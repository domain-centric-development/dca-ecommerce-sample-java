# The 201 answers the price as sent (14.995), not rounded (15.00) — how is the adopted scenario taken?

## Question
The story is `status: adopted`: it may only describe behaviour the shop already has, and adoption plans no
production change. Scenario `a-price-is-rounded-half-up-to-cents` says "Then the shop answers 201 with `price`
15.00". Today's code does not do that:

- `src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/product/application/createproduct/CreateProductUseCase.java:89`
  puts `input.priceAmount()` — the amount as the client sent it — into `CreateProductResult`, not the amount of
  the `Price` it built at line 67.
- `src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/product/adapter/incoming/api/ProductDtoConverter.java:35`
  copies it into `ProductDto.price`; no Jackson setting rounds it (`src/main/resources` has none).
- So the body carries `price` 14.995. The rounding itself is real: `Money.java:29` (`setScale(2, HALF_UP)`) holds
  15.00 for the pricing context, and the catalogue card (`product/catalog.pug:24`) shows "15.00 EUR" — the
  scenario's second clause holds.

A characterization test for the first clause cannot be green, so the scenario is not adoptable as written.
(The replay source `API-05` may come from the .NET sample answering 15.00; not checked from here — if so it is a
difference between the two samples.)

## Options
- a: Make the story a build story (`status` from `adopted` to a released one): the result carries the amount of
  the `Price` the use case built, so the 201 answers 15.00. One line of production code, a red test first; the
  other six scenarios stay characterizations.
- b: Take the clause "with `price` 15.00" out of this story (keep "answers 201" and the card) and note under
  `## Out of scope` that the 201 echoes the price as sent; the gap goes to `TODO.md` or a story of its own.
- c: Reword the clause to what the shop does: "answers 201 with `price` 14.995" — records the echo as agreed
  behaviour.

## Recommendation
a — the story's own goal is that no fraction of a cent reaches anyone, and an answer that echoes 14.995 is the one
place it still does; b if the adopted story must stay change-free, with the fix as its own story. c fixes a defect
in the spec.

## Answer
answer: a, as a story of its own — a price is a decimal with two places, rounded by the commercial rule (half up), the 201 included. The change story created-product-answers-with-its-rounded-price makes the answer carry the rounded price; this story depends on it and stays adopted as written.
