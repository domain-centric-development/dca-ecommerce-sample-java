# The happy path's Given cannot happen in this shop — which refusal after the authorization does the story use?

## Question

The happy path needs the checkout replaced by a new checkout while the provider authorizes. In this shop that
cannot happen: the confirmation runs its whole authorization inside `inCartSession`
(`src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/checkout/application/checkoutcompletion/confirmcheckout/ConfirmCheckoutUseCase.java:110`),
and the replacement takes the same lock (`src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/checkout/application/session/startcheckout/StartCheckoutUseCase.java:130`,
`src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/checkout/application/shared/CheckoutSessionRepository.java:28`).
A replacement started during the authorization waits until the order is placed, so the first checkout's order is
placed and the Then fails. No test can be made red-then-green as written. The story was taken over from the .NET
sample, where the replacement is not serialized this way.

A refusal after the authorization is reachable here by other means: during the authorization the customer changes
the delivery (or the way to pay) in a second tab. `SubmitDelivery` is not serialized, the review at confirmation
no longer matches what was shown, and `CheckoutSession.confirm` refuses with `OrderChangedSinceReviewException`
(`src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/checkout/domain/model/CheckoutSession.java:445`). Today that
refusal releases only the stock (`ConfirmCheckoutUseCase.java:142-147`). The e2e test can provoke it with a provider
stub that answers after a fixed delay (under the 2 s timeout) while the test changes the delivery.

## Options
- a: Change the happy path's second Given to "while the provider authorizes the 39.99 EUR, the customer chooses another shipping option for the same checkout".
  The Then stays the same: the review shows the order-changed refusal it shows today, and the provider receives the cancel. Costs a rewording of the story; the build stays as small as planned.
- b: Keep the replacement and stop serializing the authorization against it, so the authorization runs outside `inCartSession`.
  This changes agreed behaviour (ADR-041's serialization) beyond what the story asks, and needs its own changed expectation.
- c: Supersede the story in this shop: the refusal after the authorization is out of reach here except for technical failures.
  The finding the-payment-is-authorized-at-place-order#3 then stays open.

## Recommendation
a — it keeps the story's rule and its Then, gives the cancellation a refusal a test can bring about in this shop, and changes no agreed behaviour.
