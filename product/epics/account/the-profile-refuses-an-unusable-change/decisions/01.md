# Two adopted scenarios name sentences the profile page does not show — how should the story change?

## Question

The story is `status: adopted`, so every scenario must describe behaviour the shop already has. Four hold:
`taken-address-is-refused-in-the-profile`, `malformed-address-is-refused-in-the-profile`,
`unreadable-date-is-refused-in-the-profile` and `refused-change-saves-nothing`. Two do not. The page answers with
other sentences than the ones these scenarios name:

| Scenario | Story expects | Page shows today |
|---|---|---|
| missing-date-is-refused-in-the-profile | Date of birth is required | Please enter a date like 1990-05-17 |
| future-date-is-refused-in-the-profile | Date of birth cannot be in the future | Please enter a date of birth that is not in the future. |

The page reads a blank date field the same way as an unreadable one, so it shows the format sentence. The
sentences the story expects are the exception messages of `UsableDateOfBirth` and of the API's `RegisterRequest`.
The shops' refusal wording says such a message never reaches a page.

## Options

1. **Reword the two scenarios to what the page shows.** `missing-date-…` expects "Please enter a date like
   1990-05-17", and `future-date-…` expects "Please enter a date of birth that is not in the future." The story stays
   adopted, as decided for the registration refusals (`registration-refuses-an-unusable-form-01`, WP-107).
2. **Take the two scenarios out into a new story that is built, not adopted.** That story makes the profile page
   name a missing date on its own and use the story's wording. It needs new rows in
   `docs/architecture/refusal-wording.md`, which both shops share, and the same change in the .NET twin.
3. **Make the whole story a build story.** The four scenarios that already hold could get no red test, so the
   test gate would refuse them.

## Recommendation

Option 1. It follows the answer already given for every replay story: an adopted story describes what the shop
does, worded as `refusal-wording.md` says. The story's "so that" (never half changed) still holds either way.

## Evidence

- `src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/account/adapter/incoming/web/SubmittedDate.java:29`: a blank field is no date.
- `src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/account/adapter/incoming/web/ProfilePageController.java:141`: shown as `NOT_A_DATE`.
- `src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/account/adapter/incoming/web/ProfilePageController.java:55`: the future-date sentence.
- `docs/architecture/refusal-wording.md:89`: the profile's sentences for both refusals (line 92 for the future date).
- `src/main/java/dev/domaincentric/sample/ecommerce/account/domain/specification/UsableDateOfBirth.java:39`: the messages the story quotes (line 42 for the future date).
- `product/epics/account/registration-refuses-an-unusable-form/decisions/01.md`: the same question, answered with option 1.

## Answer
answer: 1 — missing-date-is-refused-in-the-profile expects "Please enter a date like 1990-05-17" and future-date-is-refused-in-the-profile "Please enter a date of birth that is not in the future.". The story stays adopted; the story text is already changed, in both shops and the replay source alike.
